Questions

What owners usually want to know.

Is this an inspection of my website?

No. This inquiry is for an in-person review of your restaurant. Website accessibility is a separate service we can discuss if useful.

Will I know the price before booking?

Yes. We discuss your location and needs, then confirm the proposed scope, price, and scheduling before you decide to book.

What if I already received a complaint or demand letter?

Let us know on the form and call us if you need a prompt response. An inspection does not replace advice from a qualified attorney.

Does an inspection guarantee ADA compliance?

No. The inspection is informational. It does not guarantee compliance or prevent claims, and it is not a government inspection or legal advice.
