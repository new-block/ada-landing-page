05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you start?

Is this only for restaurants?

No. We welcome inquiries from Los Angeles businesses serving the public, including offices, shops, hospitality businesses, and other customer-facing spaces.

Is a website review the same as an on-site review?

No. They look at different customer experiences. You can ask about one or both, and we’ll confirm the appropriate scope before any work begins.

Is this a certified CASp inspection?

A California CASp inspection is a specific service performed by a state-certified specialist. Ask us about the available scope and credentials before booking if you need a formal CASp inspection.

Will a review guarantee compliance?

No review can guarantee full compliance or prevent claims. We’ll discuss what the proposed review covers and what it does not.

Do I need to book right now?

No. Send an inquiry, talk with us about your needs, and decide after we confirm the proposed scope, price, and timing.
